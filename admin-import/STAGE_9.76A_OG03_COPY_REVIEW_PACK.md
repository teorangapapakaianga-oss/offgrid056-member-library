## Why This Matters

A resilience improvement only helps if your household can afford it without putting essentials at risk. A water tank, a battery system or an insulation upgrade is a good step only when it fits your real situation.

This worksheet helps you work out how much your household can realistically set aside, and how fast. It does not tell you what to buy, and it does not ask you to spend.

Use it with your own priorities, for example those from your Home Resilience Scorecard or your Resilience Product Wishlist. You might focus on one of Air, Water, Shelter, Food or Energy, or on more than one. You do not need to cover all five.

Spending on resilience should move your household toward more independence, one practical step at a time. Protect essential household needs first when deciding what you can allocate.

**The Honesty Rule** (boxed)

Use realistic figures. Do not count money your household needs for essentials, debt payments or unexpected costs. It is better to start small and keep going than to stretch too far and stop. A low figure is still a useful result.

This worksheet is for personal planning only and is not financial advice.

## Step 3: Choose a Realistic Spending Position

These are planning positions, not levels, ratings or financial assessments. None is better than another, and your position can change as your situation changes. Tick the one that fits your own figures today.

**My Spending Position**

  *Tick one and write why*

- ☐ Protect Essentials First — Essentials, debts or a safety buffer need your money right now. Focus on steps that use what you already have, such as learning, planning and checking your home. Come back to this when things change.

- ☐ Small Steps — You can set aside a little at a time. Choose small, practical improvements, and finish one before you start the next.

- ☐ Planned Upgrades — You can plan and save toward a specific improvement. Decide what you want, find out what it costs, and build up to it before you commit.

- ☐ A Larger Project — You have enough set aside, or a clear way to build it, to take on a bigger improvement. Get written quotes and a plan first, and keep your essentials protected.
