## 4. Privacy, boundary and pressure (items 17 to 19)
- **Personal data:** the prompts ask a member to disclose, to a facilitator and (for the win) a group, their **property type, budget tier, lowest-scoring pillar, highest risk, and a personal blocker**, plus a call date. No name, email, phone, address, health or contact field. **Unnecessary for a self-use sheet:** everything in the Context block is *shared outward*; the budget tier is financial information; the win is explicitly shared. In any rewrite, nothing here should be sent anywhere by the Library (it stores no member data), and context fields would need to be optional and member-defined.
- **Professional boundary:** every prompt is an open question; no legal, medical, financial, electrical or structural subject is named, and no advice is given. The "context for the facilitator" invites property and budget advice from a person, which the Library does not provide.
- **Pressure language:** "**Submit before the call**", "**Get maximum value**", "**Submit 1 to 3**", "**24 hours before the call**", "**My Win This Week (share with the group)**" and "specific blocker I am **stuck** on" (a deadline, an optimisation framing and a performance-sharing prompt). No "must", "mandatory" or shame wording. A modern version would be optional and neutral.
