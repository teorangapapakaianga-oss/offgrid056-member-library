### Why This Matters
Your property situation shapes which changes are yours to make, which may need someone else’s agreement, and which you need to check first. Knowing that before you spend or build keeps your resilience plan realistic.

This worksheet helps you record your situation and list what to check before you make changes. It does not tell you what you may or may not do. That depends on your property, your agreements and where you live, so use this worksheet to decide what to check and who to ask.

It supports your resilience plan and, if it applies to you, your off-grid plans. More independence starts with knowing what you control and what you need to confirm.

> **BEFORE YOU BEGIN**
> This worksheet is for personal planning only. It is not legal, building or professional advice, and nothing you tick or write here is a conclusion about what is allowed. Leave anything you do not know blank and come back to it.
