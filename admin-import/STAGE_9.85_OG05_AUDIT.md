## 2 · Purpose

A **one-page framework reference with a short ranking exercise**, from the old 30-Day Programme (Week 1, Day 5). It does three things:
1. **Teaches** the five systems (Air, Water, Shelter, Food, Energy), each with a one-line "this pillar covers…" and a five-item checklist (25 items).
2. **Ranks emergency priority** in a table with a "Survival Threshold" column and the "Rule of 3s".
3. **Asks the member to rank** the five pillars weakest to strongest, using their Scorecard (OG-01) and Risk Identifier (OG-02) results.

**Primary output:** reference and education (a framework to "return to when overwhelmed"), with programme navigation around it. It is not an assessment (no scoring), not a plan and not action planning. Its only member output is a five-line ranking.
