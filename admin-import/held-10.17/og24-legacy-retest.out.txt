== PROFESSIONAL / CREDENTIAL TERMS (per unit)
  Purpose: Resilience projects span plumbing, electrical, structural, and consent work.
  Doing everything yourself is not always legal, safe, or efficient.
  Licensed Required?
  Solar PV installation (&gt;120V DC or grid-tied) No Yes — electrician Always.
  Illegal and dangerous otherwise.
  Battery system installation No Yes — electrician Fire and electrocution risk.
  Always licensed.
  Plumbing to mains water No Yes — plumber Backflow and contamination risk.
  Structural building work No Yes — LBP builder Anything affecting load-bearing elements.
  Heat pump installation (split system) No Yes — refrigeration engineer Refrigerant handling requires certified technician.
  Wood burner installation No Yes — solid fuel installer Building consent and certified flue work required.
  Permaculture design & soil testing Yes No Complex slopes, drainage issues, or food safety certification.
  Septic or greywater system No Yes — drainlayer Public health and environmental regulations apply.
  Licensed Building Practitioner (LBP) — Builder / Carpenter
  What they do: Structural work, weather tightness, consent-compliant building.
  When you need one: Any project over $33,000 (Consumer Guarantees Act threshold), structural alterations, or work requiring building consent.
  How to verify: Search the LBP register at lbp.website — valid licence and category match required.
  Registered Electrician
  How to verify: Check Electrical Workers Registration Board (EWRB) registration.
  Certifying Plumber / Gasfitter / Drainlayer
  Energy Auditor / Home Performance Assessor
  How to verify: Look for EECA-recognised assessors or Home Performance Advisor accreditation.
  Architect / Architectural Designer
  What they do: Design, documentation, and consent navigation for structural or aesthetic changes.
  When you need one: New builds, major renovations, or complex consent applications.
  How to verify: New Zealand Registered Architects Board (NZRAB) or ADNZ membership.
  Environmental / Ecological Consultant
  You now know exactly which projects require licensed professionals, how to verify their credentials, and how to brief them for accurate quotes.
