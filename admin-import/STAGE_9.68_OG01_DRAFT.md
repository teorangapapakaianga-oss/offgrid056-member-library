### 1. Title and intro

**Cover:** chip *Start Here* · label *OffGrid056 Member Library* · title **Home Resilience Scorecard** · subtitle *Assess your household across Air, Water, Shelter, Food and Energy, identify the foundations that need the most attention, and choose practical next steps for building greater resilience and independence.*

**Page header:** OffGrid056 | Home Resilience Scorecard — *Start Here*

> **HOW TO USE THIS SCORECARD** — Rate your household from 1 to 10 on each question in Part A. Be honest — this is for your eyes only. A low score isn't a failure; it shows you where to start. Allow about 20 minutes.

### 2. Self-assessment explanation

> **ABOUT THIS SCALE** — This is an OffGrid056 self-assessment scale. It helps you think about your own household and decide where to start. It is not scientifically validated, it is not approved by any government or agency, it does not predict safety, and it is not a guarantee of preparedness.

### 3. Scoring instructions

> **How To Score** — For each question, choose a number from 1 to 10. A 1 means it is not in place at all, a 5 means it is partly in place, and a 10 means it is fully in place and you feel confident about it. Score what is true today, not what you plan to do. Then add your two scores for each foundation to get a result out of 20.

### 7. Household Considerations (unscored prompts, each with a blank line)

> **HOUSEHOLD CONSIDERATIONS** — These are not scored. Use them to note anyone or anything in your home that needs particular planning.
>
> - Communication — how we would stay in touch with each other and with others if phones or the internet were down
> - Children, older people, or anyone with disability or mobility needs
> - Essential medications or other health-related needs, including any equipment that needs power
> - Sanitation — how we would manage toilets and hygiene if services were interrupted
> - Heating and cooking fuel, where it applies to our home
> - Pets or animals, where they apply to our household
