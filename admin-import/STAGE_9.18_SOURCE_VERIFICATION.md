## 2. FENZ heater-clearance guidance — VERIFIED_LIVE, wording corrected

**Pages read live (22 Sep 2026):**

- *Indoor fires and heaters* (as above): *"Heaters have at least a metre of clear space surrounding them while in
  use."* · *"Clothing and other items are not left to dry on top of heaters."*
- *Keep a metre from the heater*, a news release dated **14 July 2020**
  (https://www.fireandemergency.nz/incidents-and-news/news-and-media/keep-a-metre-from-the-heater/): *"Clothes,
  bedding or anything that could catch fire should always be at least one metre away from a heat source."*

**Difference from the earlier draft:** it said *"at least one metre from any heater **or fire**"*. The current
guidance page puts the metre around **heaters**. The 2020 release says **"a heat source"**. The wording now uses
both sources' own terms and adds the "not on top of heaters" rule. It does not claim more than either page says.

## 4. Final NZ wording (after verification)

**Wood burners and open fires** (HIGH) — VERIFIED_LIVE, awaiting approval

> **Have your chimney flue cleaned every year** — put a reminder in your calendar to have it done before you light
> the first fire of the season. Always use a fireguard or spark guard when the fire is lit.
> Give heaters at least a metre of clear space while they are in use, never dry clothing on top of a heater, and
> keep clothes, bedding and anything else that could catch fire **at least one metre** from any heat source.
> Let ashes cool before you get rid of them: empty them into a metal bin and pour water over any hot ashes — ashes
> can take up to five days to cool completely.

**Stay off the roof** (CRITICAL) — VERIFIED_LIVE, awaiting approval

> **You do not need to climb onto the roof to complete this worksheet.** Take measurements from your house plans or
> other building documents, ask the installers who are quoting to measure the roof for you, or estimate from the
> ground — and leave blank anything you cannot fill in safely.
> Falls are the most common cause of injury in Aotearoa. If you do use a ladder, use the right size and type, make
> sure it is balanced and fully extended, and avoid the top rungs. If a job is too tricky or needs special gear,
> get a professional to do it.

**Never bring it inside** (CRITICAL) — last sentence corrected, awaiting approval

> …never use it in a bedroom, bathroom, caravan or tent; keep internal doors and at least one window open; have it
> serviced by a qualified person at least once a year; and use it for as short a time as you can. **LPG cabinet
> heaters are not recommended in homes where someone has a respiratory condition.**

The yearly service interval now has three NZ sources behind it: Health NZ, WorkSafe (cabinet heaters, "every 12
months") and FENZ (LPG heaters, "serviced within the last 12 months").

**Unchanged:**

- **Batteries and electrical: OWNER-APPROVED.**
- **Carbon monoxide:** verified, awaiting approval.
